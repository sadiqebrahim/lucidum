<h1 class="article__title">Data deletion instructions</h1>
</header>
<div class="prose" markdown="1">

The Lucidum publishing tool stores very little about anyone other than our own account: only the public username and text of comments left on **@lucidum.science** posts, and only until we reply, for at most 30 days (see our [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})).

**To have your data deleted:**

1. Send a direct message to [@lucidum.science on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/lucidum.science/) saying "Delete my data", from the account the data relates to.
2. We will delete any comments or other data from that account in our storage within **30 days** and confirm by reply.

You can also simply delete your comment on Instagram; once it is deleted, the tool can no longer read it, and any stored copy is removed within 30 days.
